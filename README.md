# Global Terrorism Data Analysis

This project analyzes global terrorism data to identify trends, patterns, and insights across time and regions. The aim is to explore geographic distributions, attack types, and casualty figures to better understand global security threats from a data-driven perspective.

## 📊 Objective

- Analyze historical data on global terrorist incidents
- Identify trends by year, country, and region
- Visualize the most frequent attack types and target groups
- Highlight countries most affected by terrorism over time

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Terrorism trends have shifted over the decades**, with noticeable spikes during specific geopolitical events.
- **The Middle East and South Asia** are among the most affected regions in terms of attack frequency and casualties.
- **Bombings and armed assaults** are the most common forms of attack globally.
- **Civilians and government institutions** are consistently the most targeted groups.
- A small number of countries account for a large share of the total incidents.
